Guidelines

Last updated: October 2, 2026

We encourage gameplay videos and live streams of our game, including monetized content and videos containing spoilers.

You do not need to ask us for permission before uploading or streaming gameplay content that follows these guidelines.

Monetization through platform features such as ads, subscriptions, memberships, and donations is allowed.

You may use music from the game as part of gameplay videos, streams, reviews, and similar content.

However, standalone uploads or redistribution of the game’s music or soundtrack are not allowed.

You may not redistribute game assets or files, or sell, license, or claim ownership of our game or any of its assets.

All content must comply with applicable laws and the rules of the platform where it is posted.

We reserve the right to request the removal of content that violates these guidelines or infringes our rights.

These guidelines are subject to change without notice.

Thank you for reading!
